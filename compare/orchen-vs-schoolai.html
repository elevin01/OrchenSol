Orchen vs SchoolAI

Both platforms put AI in front of students. Compare how each connects classroom context, guided work, and the insight adults use to support learning.

Updated November 3, 2026

If your school wants teachers to configure AI environments per assignment, with live visibility into every conversation as it happens, SchoolAI is well-designed for that workflow. The teacher Spaces model gives classroom autonomy and real-time monitoring in a single package.

Orchen connects your school's curriculum, guided student work, and a growing picture of learning. Teachers build assignments from class materials, while the school controls visibility for staff and families.

Honest Assessment

Where SchoolAI is strong

These are genuine strengths, not soft-pedaled. If any of these are your primary need, SchoolAI deserves serious consideration.

  • Teacher Spaces, Sidekick, and PowerUps

    Teachers create or remix Spaces around a lesson, with their own instructions and objectives. Dot supports Space-based activities; PowerUps add interactive tools. Paid Scale plans also include a student portal and Sidekick for use beyond individual Space sessions. How Spaces work

  • Mission Control, real-time oversight dashboard

    SchoolAI's Mission Control gives teachers live visibility into every student conversation as it happens. Teachers can intervene, redirect, or use conversations as teaching moments in real time, not after the fact.

  • Over 1 million educators and 200,000+ teacher-created Spaces

    SchoolAI reports over 1 million educators using its platform. Its Spaces guide describes more than 200,000 teacher-created activities to browse and adapt.

  • Strong compliance posture and multi-layer safety

    SchoolAI documents critical alerts and configurable routing for student safety concerns. Its compliance page lists SOC 2 Type 2, FERPA, COPPA, FIPPA, and 1EdTech status, with ESSA Tier 3 validation. Its data protection page says student data is not used to train AI models.

How Orchen Approaches This

Where Orchen is different

Not better in every case. Different in ways that matter for specific schools.

  • Grounded in the class

    Orchen connects the tutor to the class's own materials, current unit, assignments, and learning context. Teachers can build assignments from those same sources, and the resulting work feeds back into student and class insight.

  • The school decides what visibility looks like

    Orchen turns student work into role-appropriate learning insight. The school controls what teachers, advisors, and families can see, with parent visibility configured separately.

  • Evidence tied to the work

    Assignments connect guided work to curriculum objectives and standards. Schools can track what students encountered, engaged with, and demonstrated, with evidence linked to the work.

  • Source conversation deletion within 7 days

    Orchen's architecture deletes the raw transcript within 7 days of generation. The derived insight is retained separately. This is a deliberate data minimization decision, not just a compliance checkbox.

  • 5-tier parent visibility model

    Orchen's parent access is structured in five tiers with school-mediated defaults, allowing schools to calibrate how much parents see without opening full transcript access by default.

Decision Guide

How to decide

Choose SchoolAI if…

  • You want teachers to configure AI per assignment, with their own instructions and persona
  • Real-time live monitoring of student conversations is your primary safety need
  • You're looking for a tool that lives in the classroom workflow, not at the school administrative level
  • You want to evaluate teacher-designed Spaces through a limited free trial

Choose Orchen if…

  • You want guided learning grounded in your own classes, materials, and assignments
  • You want useful teacher and advisor insight, with visibility set by your school
  • A clear data deletion policy is a priority for your board or legal counsel
  • You want standards evidence connected to the work students actually do

Consider both if…

  • SchoolAI handles classroom AI per assignment; Orchen handles the school-wide advisor insight and student tutoring environment. For schools that want depth on both axes, they're not necessarily competing.
Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

Can SchoolAI customize the AI's personality per school?

Yes. Teachers can configure instructions and personas for individual Spaces. SchoolAI’s Scale plan also lists district-wide instructions, preferred standards, and learning frameworks. See the plan details.

Orchen sets the AI’s identity and policies at the school level, with that configuration applying across students and sessions.

Does SchoolAI delete student conversation data?

Yes. SchoolAI says districts can request deletion of an individual student’s data or their platform data as a whole. Its zero-data-retention agreements with AI providers apply to model processing; SchoolAI itself retains platform records for learning. Review its data protection practices and your school’s agreement for the applicable retention and deletion terms.

Orchen deletes source conversation transcripts within seven days by default. Derived learning profiles and narratives follow the school’s retention policy. Read Orchen’s retention policy.

Can advisors or counselors see longitudinal student patterns in SchoolAI?

SchoolAI documents a Student Insights dashboard that combines session history with individual Sidekick chats and alerts. Access is described for rostered teachers and organization admins. Its public guide does not establish a dedicated counselor workflow; confirm permissions and cross-subject reporting with SchoolAI. Read the Student Insights guide.

Orchen gives teachers class and individual learning insight, and advisors longitudinal context and weekly narratives. Access follows the school’s visibility settings.

Does Orchen have a teacher Spaces equivalent?

Orchen is organized around classes and assignments. Teachers add materials to a class Knowledge Bank and create guided assignments from those sources. Student work feeds class and individual insight, within the school's AI and visibility settings.

Which is better for a 1:1 device school?

Both work in the browser on existing school devices. Orchen also offers an optional pre-configured managed device layer for network-level enforcement. A pilot can help your team evaluate class materials, guided assignments, visibility settings, and whether that optional layer is useful.

Can we pilot Orchen alongside SchoolAI?

Yes. You can evaluate Orchen alongside SchoolAI with a free, six-week single-class pilot. Your school can keep its existing tools while testing Orchen’s guided learning and teacher/advisor insight.

Arrange a pilot with Emil. We agree on the scope before students begin, and your school reviews and finalizes its student data terms with us before any student logs in.
