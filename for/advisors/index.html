How It Works

The longer a school uses Orchen, the richer each profile becomes.

Every chat session, writing assignment, and quiz updates a private institutional profile of how that student processes information — their pace, their preferred approach, where they disengage, where they push back.

You see this as a narrative, not a data dump. After a year, you have something no grade report or college essay can give you: a record of how each student actually thinks.

Independent effort

Safety

Concerning content reaches you — not your inbox.

Orchen flags concerning student content automatically and routes it to your designated counselor with a 24-hour urgency indicator. Every flag moves through a full lifecycle — open, reviewing, resolved or escalated — and every step is logged.

You see only what's been shared with you. Source conversations are deleted within seven days. You never have access to raw transcripts unless the student has shared a session directly.

How Visibility Works

You see derived insight. Never raw transcripts.

Orchen does not give advisors a window into student conversations. It synthesizes those conversations into narratives, learning signals, and concept-level profiles — and that synthesis is what you see.

Students have a private workspace that no staff member can access. What advisors see is scoped, synthesized, and logged every time it's accessed.
