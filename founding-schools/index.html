The Straight Version

We're early. Here's why that works in your favor.

Orchen doesn't have a hundred school logos to show you. What it has is a working platform, an architecture built around student privacy from day one, and a founder who will personally configure, support, and adapt the product for the schools that join first.

Established vendors offer track records. Founding partnerships offer something different: influence. The schools in this program will decide what the parent digest emphasizes, how the advisor narrative reads, what the writing rubrics default to. By the time Orchen is a hundred-school product, it will carry the fingerprints of the first five.

If your school wants a finished, audited, reference-checked product, we'll honestly tell you to wait a year. If your school wants to design its AI environment rather than inherit one, read on.

The Terms

What founding schools get. What we ask in return.

You get

Direct access to the founder

No account managers, no support queues. You work with the person who built the platform — during setup, during the pilot, and when something needs to change. Configuration sessions, faculty onboarding, and the security walkthrough are all run personally.

You get

Configuration built with you, not delivered to you

Your school's AI identity, tone, mechanical-feedback policy, parent visibility defaults, and crisis routing are configured together in working sessions. The system prompt your students experience reflects decisions your school actually made.

You get

Founding pricing, locked

Founding schools receive pilot-phase pricing that is held at renewal — the rate you join at is the rate you keep as the program grows. Pilot cohorts (roughly 30–40 students and a few teachers) run at minimal cost to the school.

You get

A real voice in the roadmap

Founding schools meet with the founder monthly during the school year. Feature requests from this group are weighted above everything else. This isn't a feedback form; it's a seat at the table.

We ask

A genuine cohort, not a checkbox

A pilot only proves anything if students actually use it. We ask for a committed cohort — one or two classes, a few engaged teachers — and a school-side point person who can keep the loop tight.

We ask

Honest feedback, including the uncomfortable kind

We'd rather hear that the advisor narrative missed the point than have it politely ignored. Founding schools agree to a short structured check-in every few weeks during the pilot.

We ask

A reference — only if we earn it

If the pilot succeeds by your measures, we ask permission to name your school and, ideally, a quote from your team. If it doesn't succeed, you owe us nothing, and your data export is waiting.

The Path

How a founding pilot runs.

Scoping call · 45 minutes

A walkthrough of the live product against your school's situation: which subjects, which grade band, which teachers, what success would look like by your measures. If it's not a fit, we say so here.

Review & agreement · 1–2 weeks

Your technical and legal reviewers get the security walkthrough, the DPA, and direct answers. The pilot agreement covers data ownership, the export right, and exit terms in plain language.

Configuration · 1 week

Working sessions to set your school's AI identity, visibility defaults, crisis routing, and feedback policies. Accounts are provisioned for the cohort; teachers get a hands-on onboarding session.

Cohort pilot · 6–8 weeks

One or two classes use Orchen for real coursework — chat tutoring, writing assignments, quizzes. Teachers watch the insight layer fill in. Check-ins every two weeks; fixes and adjustments ship during the pilot, not after it.

Review & decide

A joint review against the success measures defined in step one — engagement data, teacher experience, advisor narrative quality. Then your school decides: expand, continue as-is, or stop with a full data export. No pressure mechanics; the data makes the case or it doesn't.

Common Questions

Before you apply.

How many founding schools are you taking? ⌄

A handful — deliberately. Each founding school gets real founder time during configuration and the pilot, and that doesn't scale past a small number of schools per term. When the cohort is full, applications roll to the following term.

What does the pilot cost? ⌄

Pilot cohorts run at minimal cost to the school — the founding program is structured so that price is never the reason a school can't evaluate properly. Full-school pricing is discussed only after a pilot has earned the conversation, and founding schools keep their founding rate.

What if it doesn't work for us? ⌄

You stop. The pilot agreement includes a clean exit: a full structured export of your data, deletion of everything else, and no early-termination mechanics. A founding program built on lock-in wouldn't deserve the name.

Can we pilot Orchen alongside tools we already use? ⌄

Yes. Many schools run teacher-productivity tools like MagicSchool, or classroom platforms like SchoolAI, and pilot Orchen for the student-tutoring and advisor-insight side. See our comparison pages for where the lines fall.

Do we need special devices or IT setup? ⌄

No. Orchen runs in the browser on your existing fleet — iPads, MacBooks, Chromebooks. A managed device layer with network-level enforcement is available as an optional add-on for schools that want it, but no pilot requires it.
