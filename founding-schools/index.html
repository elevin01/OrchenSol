The Straight Version

Founding schools shape what comes next.

We’re intentionally small this year. Every founding school works directly with the founder on setup, in-person training, and support. That level of access is reserved for this cohort.

The founding cohort will help shape the product. Your school can help decide what the parent digest emphasizes, how the advisor narrative reads, and what the writing rubrics default to. The product will grow from the work we do together in these first schools.

If your school wants to inherit an AI environment someone else designed, any vendor will sell you one. If your school wants to design its own, that is exactly what the founding cohort is for.
The Terms

What founding schools get. What we ask in return.

The founding cohort will help shape the product. Here's exactly what your school receives, and the short list of what we ask for so the pilot proves something real.

You get

Direct access to the founder

No account managers, no support queues. You work with the person who built the platform, during setup, the pilot, and whenever something needs to change. Configuration, onboarding, and the security walkthrough are run personally.

You get

Configuration built with you

Your AI identity, tone, mechanical-feedback policy, parent visibility defaults, and crisis routing are set together in working sessions. The system prompt your students meet reflects decisions your school actually made.

You get

30% off your first 3 years

Choose to continue after the pilot and your school receives 30% off its license, with the agreed founding rate held for all three paid years. That rate is exclusive to the founding cohort and won’t be offered again.

You get

A real voice in the roadmap

Founding schools meet with the founder monthly during the year, and their feedback shapes priorities. Your teachers help decide what comes next; later schools benefit from the work you helped shape.

We ask

A genuine cohort, not a checkbox

A pilot only proves something if students actually use it. We ask for a committed cohort, one or two classes, a few engaged teachers, and a point person who keeps the loop tight.

We ask

Honest feedback, the uncomfortable kind

We'd rather hear that the advisor narrative missed the point than have it politely ignored. Founding schools agree to a short structured check-in every few weeks.

We ask

An evaluation guided by your school

Define success with your team before the pilot begins, then review the results together. Your school reviews its student data terms before student access. An independent security review is scheduled ahead of full rollout and before any paid agreement, with founding schools receiving the results first.

The Path

How a founding pilot runs.

Five steps from first call to decision, most of it measured in weeks, with the decision fully yours at the end, backed by your own data.

1
45 minutes
Scoping call

A walkthrough of the live product against your situation: subjects, grade band, teachers, and what success looks like by your measures. If it's not a fit, we say so here.

2
1–2 weeks
Review & agreement

Your school reviews and finalizes the terms with us before any student logs in. The student data terms are written around FERPA and COPPA and modeled on New York Education Law 2-d. We can use your school or diocese's own agreement. Bring your technical team for a live walkthrough of the security controls.

3
1 week
Configuration

Working sessions set your AI identity, visibility defaults, crisis routing, and feedback policies. Accounts are provisioned; teachers get a hands-on onboarding session.

4
6–8 weeks
Cohort pilot

One or two classes use Orchen for real coursework. Teachers watch the insight layer fill in. Check-ins every two weeks; fixes ship during the pilot, not after it.

5
Decision
Review & decide

A joint review against the success measures from step one. Then you decide: expand, continue as-is, or stop with a full data export. Zero pressure mechanics, your own data makes the case.

Common Questions

Before you apply.

Are founding school spots still available?

Yes. There are 3 spots remaining for spring 2027. Spots are limited so Emil can personally set up and support each school. In-person training is in January 2027, with school decisions needed by December 15, 2026.

What does the pilot cost?

The founding school pilot is free: one class for about six weeks. We agree on the pilot scope and what success looks like before it begins. If you choose to continue afterward, we agree on paid pricing before you decide. Your school receives 30% off its license, with the agreed founding rate locked in for the first three paid years.

How does the founding school discount work?

Founding schools that stay after the pilot receive 30% off their school license, with the agreed rate held for the first three paid years. That founding rate won’t be offered again. It starts when your school continues on a paid license after the pilot.

What if it doesn't work for us?

You can choose not to continue after the pilot. Your school can request a structured export and deletion of its data under the agreed student data terms and our Privacy Policy.

Can we pilot Orchen alongside tools we already use?

Yes. Your school can keep its existing tools while evaluating Orchen for student learning support and teacher and advisor insight. See our comparison pages for where the lines fall.

Do we need special devices or IT setup?

No. Orchen runs in the browser on your existing fleet, iPads, MacBooks, Chromebooks. A managed device layer with network-level enforcement is available as an optional add-on for schools that want it, but no pilot requires it.
